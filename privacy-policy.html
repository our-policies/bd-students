This policy explains what data the app Student BD: Result, MCQ, GPA (package com.nadesoft.bdschoolcollegelist), published on Google Play by NADESOFT, collects, how it is used and what choices you have.

1. Not a government app

Student BD is an independent app made by NADESOFT. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Government of Bangladesh, any education board or any university. Result links open the official sites listed in section 10. Institute data is compiled from publicly available sources.

3. Data that stays on your device

  • Marksheet vault: photos of marksheets, admit cards and certificates you add are stored only on your phone. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • SMS result inputs (exam, board, roll, year), MCQ best scores, recent searches and the offline institute database are stored locally.
  • Result by SMS: the app only prepares the message text. Your phone's SMS app sends it to 16222 and your operator's standard charge applies. We do not read or receive your SMS.

4. How we use data

To provide app features (sign-in, sync, alerts), respond to feedback, keep the app secure and show ads. We do not sell your personal data.

5. Third parties

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging) stores account, profile, bookmark and feedback data. Firebase privacy information
  • Google Sign-In, only if you use it.
  • Google AdMob shows ads and may use the advertising ID and similar identifiers. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Official result and admission websites open in an in-app browser. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the service providers above, to run the app, or if required by law. Feedback is readable only by NADESOFT.

7. Security and retention

Data is sent over HTTPS and protected by Firebase security rules so each user can access only their own profile and bookmarks. We keep account data until you delete your account. Feedback is kept as long as it is useful for improving the app.

8. Your choices

  • You can use the app without an account; only the anonymous ID is created.
  • Turn off result-day alerts in the app or in Android notification settings.
  • Reset or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings > Google > Ads.

9. Data deletion and account deletion

You can delete your account and the data linked to it at any time, inside or outside the app.

Option A: delete inside the app

  1. Open Student BD: Result, MCQ, GPA and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under "About & privacy", tap Delete account and confirm. This option is available when you are signed in.
  3. If the app asks you to log out and log back in, do so and then tap Delete account again.

If you use the app as a guest (no account), there is no account to delete in the app; use option B or simply uninstall the app.

Option B: request deletion by email (without opening the app)

Email contact@nadesoft.bd with the subject "Delete my data - Student BD" from the email address registered in the app. If you used the app without an account, tell us the app version and describe the feedback you sent, because anonymous IDs cannot be looked up by email. We will confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login account (email, Google sign-in link or anonymous ID) in Firebase Authentication
  • Your profile (name, education level, sex, location, current school, avatar, theme and language preference)
  • All your bookmarked institutes
  • Locally cached copies of the above on your device
  • Feedback messages you sent, when you ask us by email (option B)

What is not collected or kept

  • Marksheet vault photos, SMS inputs, MCQ scores and recent searches stay only on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.
  • Advertising data held by Google AdMob is managed by Google; see the Google links in section 5.
  • Backups of deleted data may remain in Firebase for a short period before they are permanently erased, and we may keep information if the law requires us to.

10. Official sources for government information

The app links to these official sites and does not host their data:

  • Education board results: educationboardresults.gov.bd
  • XI class admission: xiclassadmission.gov.bd
  • BTEB: bteb.gov.bd · Madrasah board: bmeb.gov.bd
  • DPE: dpe.gov.bd · DSHE: dshe.gov.bd · NCTB: nctb.gov.bd · NTRCA: ntrca.gov.bd
  • National University: results.nu.ac.bd · admission.nu.edu.bd
  • GST admission: gstadmission.ac.bd

11. Children

The app is for students and parents. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 beyond the anonymous ID and what they choose to enter. A parent or guardian may contact us to have it deleted.

12. Changes

We will update this page and the "Last updated" date when the policy changes.
